About Farm Vibes
International

Welcome to Farm Vibes International

Pure Ingredients.
Global Trust.

At Farm Vibes International, we combine traditional agricultural wisdom with modern extraction technologies to deliver premium plant-based ingredients like CO₂ extracts, essential oils, oleoresins, and herbal extracts.

We source directly from certified manufacturers and farmer networks, ensuring purity, sustainability, and traceability in every shipment. Every product complies with FSSAI, GMP, ISO, EU, and FDA standards.

  • Eco-friendly sourcing & fair trade
  • Verified by COA & lab reports
  • Custom packaging & export logistics
  • Supplying industries worldwide
Trusted Global Partner

🚀 Our Mission

At Farm Vibes International, our mission is to deliver excellence by providing the highest quality CO₂ extracts, essential oils, and natural ingredients that meet global standards.

We drive innovation by leveraging AI, IoT, and precision processing to ensure safe, efficient, and customized solutions worldwide. Supporting sustainability, fair trade, and ethical sourcing, we empower farmers and communities, creating shared growth and long-term value.

🔭 Our Vision

To be recognised as a trusted global partner for quality, integrity, and innovation by delivering pure, sustainable, and plant-based solutions that empower farmers, enrich industries, and enhance lives.

We envision building long-term partnerships based on trust, transparency, and sustainability — where science and farmers thrive together.

🌱 Our Expertise

From Nature to Innovation – Powered by Purity

We follow globally recognized standards (FSSAI, GMP, ISO, HACCP) to deliver ingredients that are safe, sustainable, and compliant.

With decades of expertise in essential oils, oleoresins, and natural extracts, we empower businesses worldwide with ingredients they can trust. From sourcing and processing to packaging and logistics, we maintain transparency, quality, and sustainability at every step.

Our Journey

From India’s Farms
To Global Shelves

Our story began with a vision to make India’s natural heritage accessible to the world. At Farm Vibes International, we bridge nature and science to create high-quality natural extracts for food, beverages, cosmetics, pharmaceuticals, nutraceuticals, and wellness industries.

Founded in India – the land of rich biodiversity and traditional herbal wisdom – we combine modern extraction technologies with ethical sourcing to ensure purity, potency, and sustainability.
